# Scientific Reviewers & Keyholders

## Purpose

PKL should seek scrutiny from people with relevant scientific, epistemological, and technical expertise. This list records **proposed** reviewers and stakeholders we may approach.

A person's inclusion here does **not** mean they have endorsed PKL, agreed to participate, or hold authority over the truth of any claim.

PKL's constitution is explicit that authority, reputation, institutional status, or identity must not substitute for evidence. Reviewers are therefore invited to **challenge the model**, not to act as arbiters whose names confer truth. See `CONSTITUTION.md`.

## Reviewer roles

PKL should distinguish between several forms of participation rather than creating a single class of privileged "keyholder":

| Role | Purpose | Authority over truth claims? |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific reviewer | Challenge scientific and methodological assumptions | No |
| Epistemology reviewer | Challenge the evidence/knowledge model | No |
| Technical reviewer | Review architecture, security, and reproducibility | No |
| Domain expert | Review claims within a specific field | No |
| Governance/keyholder | Help protect project governance or critical infrastructure | Limited to defined governance duties; never truth authority |
| Contributor | Submit evidence, claims, analysis, or challenges | No automatic authority |

## Engagement protocol

When approaching a proposed reviewer, the initial request should be for **critique rather than endorsement or adoption**.

Suggested request:

> We have built a small open-source prototype for representing claims, evidence, provenance and disagreement in an auditable way. Could you try to break the model and tell us where it fails?

Any person who agrees to participate should be asked what role they are comfortable with. Their name should only be moved from **Proposed** to an active reviewer/keyholder role with their consent.

## Record of participation

When someone agrees to participate, record:

- their agreed role;
- date of agreement;
- public profile or institutional affiliation where they want it included;
- relevant review scope;
- conflicts of interest where voluntarily disclosed;
- links to substantive review contributions.

Do not imply endorsement from a person's inclusion on this page alone.

## Selection principles

Future reviewers should be selected for **independence and constructive disagreement**, not simply fame or prestige. A healthy reviewer group should contain people who can challenge different parts of PKL and who do not all share the same assumptions.
